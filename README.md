# 🚀 AI GTM Copilot

**AI-Powered Go-To-Market Intelligence & CRM Automation Platform**

> Built using **n8n, Apollo MCP, Firecrawl, Groq AI, HubSpot CRM, JavaScript, and Spreadsheet Automation**

---

# 📌 Overview

AI GTM Copilot is an end-to-end Go-To-Market (GTM) automation platform that transforms a company name into actionable sales intelligence.

The workflow automatically:

* Enriches company information
* Extracts website intelligence
* Generates AI-powered GTM discovery reports
* Scores sales opportunities
* Creates personalized outreach
* Generates multi-step sales sequences
* Creates companies in HubSpot CRM
* Exports structured GTM reports to Excel

The project demonstrates how AI can significantly reduce manual sales research while improving prospect qualification and outreach quality.

Company Input

↓

Apollo MCP Company Enrichment

↓

Firecrawl Website Intelligence

↓

Merge Company Data

↓

AI GTM Discovery Agent (Groq)

↓

Parse GTM Report

↓

Lead Scoring Engine

↓

Personalized Outreach Agent

↓

Multi-Step Sales Sequence Agent

↓

HubSpot CRM Company Creation

↓

Merge Outputs

↓

Spreadsheet Report Export

↓

Excel File Generation
